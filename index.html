About

I design complex multilayer boards, including high-speed, high-frequency, analog and digital circuits, from schematic capture and component selection to hardware debugging and production. My work spans IoT, smart city and wireless products, with a focus on reliable, manufacturable and cost-effective designs.

Fiber optic modem

Designed for a company as a compact, high-speed board with four Gigabit Ethernet ports, an SFP port, PHY and VoIP circuits for phone calls and connectivity, and two mini PCIe slots that let the company connect any Wi-Fi module of its choice. Delivered in 6-layer and 8-layer versions.

Gas meter reader

Designed an ultra-low-power, ultra-compact board that reads gas meters over LoRaWAN. With a sleep current of about 1.8 µA, it runs for 4 years on a single coin cell, sending one report per day.

  • LoRa
  • LoRaWAN
  • Ultra-low-power design
  • Ultra-compact design
  • Antenna design

ECG device

Designed the hardware for a compact, low-power heart monitor that tracks heart rate and detects dangerous conditions. In an emergency it sends the patient's location and places a call automatically.

  • ESP32
  • GSM
  • Heart-rate measurement
  • Compact design
  • Low-power design

Smart city audio system

Designed an integrated hardware platform for a smart city audio system, installed in streets and squares. A single design combines 4G and 2G connectivity, on-device storage for audio messages, high-quality I2S audio playback and a custom amplifier stage. From a central dashboard, operators can remotely play any stored message on all units at once or only on selected ones.
